About Us

Tucked along a quiet Southern street, The Black Magnolia blends the charm of a well-loved library with the warmth of a neighborhood café.

Inside, the glow of vintage lamps softens the dark wood shelves, and the scent of brewed coffee mingles with vanilla and pastry.

It’s a place made for slow mornings, rainy afternoons, and anyone who finds comfort in stories, soft lighting, and the steady hum of a welcoming space.
